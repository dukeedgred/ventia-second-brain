Transport asset data: what we have today, where it goes, and the value it unlocks, across road, structures, tunnels, rail and fleet. Road themes follow the Austroads RADS v4 standard, extended across the wider transport base; obligations map to RAMC CN-25949.

Everything below is a best-guess draft for the team to correct. Click any status to re-rate it, edit source and owner in place, then export to CSV. Re-rating flows through to the roadmap and value-case readiness.
Databricks QA pass (2026-07-02): Road/RAMC datasets below were cross-checked against the Second Brain Databricks knowledge base and corrected where overstated or contradicted — see each card's flag text. Databricks coverage is currently RAMC/road-only (Asset Vision asset_vision_ven_gen7 / transport_ramc, plus the shared transport SAP-adjacent schema); it has no documented tables for motorway/tunnel, rail, port or airport assets, so every dataset tagged to those areas remains interview-sourced and independently unverified. Two road datasets (WRK-08, CON-11) already exist in Databricks but were missing from the original stocktake and have been added.
