What You Are Seeing

Overview dots are not exact assets. They are 0.05-degree grid cells aggregated in Databricks and cached locally for speed.

Exact detail queries Databricks on demand and draws the selected WKT lines, polygons or points. After loading detail, the app switches to exact-only mode.

Use row cap and simplify controls when the layer is heavy. Higher simplify values draw fewer coordinate points.
